# Taste

Run this checklist before a draft or a design is handed to the owner. Run every section that fits the piece. Skip a section that does not apply, and say that you skipped it.

A miss means fix it, or stop and ask. Do not hand the piece over with a miss. Passing this list is not a yes to send, post, schedule, enroll, or launch. The owner is the final reviewer.

Read `brand/AGENTS.md` first. The values you check against live in `brand.local/`. If a required file there is missing or blank, stop before this list. Never guess.

## Copy

- The lines follow `brand.local/voice.md`: tone, words in use, words out of use, banned words, do and don't.
- Example lines are ones the owner wrote in that file. None are invented here.
- A claim, a customer, a metric, or a product fact stays out unless `brand.local/identity.md` names a proof source that contains it.
- The closing line is a next step the owner already named. The keyword on a lead magnet is one the owner named.

## Design

Skip this section when the piece has no image.

- Colors, type, spacing, and grid match `brand.local/style.md` and `brand.local/tokens.css`.
- No color, font, or margin appears that those files do not name.
- A browser fallback, such as Canvas or a generic sans-serif, is not a brand. Do not treat it as the design.
- Labels do not overlap. Connectors do not cross or cover one another.
- A logo, photo, or icon is a file `brand.local/assets/README.md` lists, used only where that list allows.
- Portrait pixels come only from a photo the owner supplied. No generated, retouched, or replaced face or body.

## Motion

Skip this section when the piece has no GIF or MP4.

- The still and the motion come from one HTML source.
- The owner has accepted that still. Motion was not exported before that.
- The numbered infographic is on screen, including the closing line, by about 2.3 seconds, then one gentle loop of about 6 seconds whose start and end match.
- A still PNG fallback exists.
- Duration, frame rate, and file size the owner did not write in `brand.local/style.md` stay marked to-check. They are not platform rules.

## Accessibility

- The words are real text, not baked into a picture.
- On an image, the text color and the background are the pair `brand.local/style.md` names. If that pair is blank, ask. Do not invent a contrast color.
- Type is large enough to read at the export size, and at the phone width in `brand.local/style.md`. If that width is blank, ask. Do not invent a pixel width.

## No invented numbers

- Every figure on the piece has a source named in `brand.local/identity.md` or in the brief. If it does not, take it off.
- Unknown stays unknown. A missing count is not zero.
