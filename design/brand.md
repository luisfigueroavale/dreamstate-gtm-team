# Brand rules

This file is a blank form. It is for any team. It holds no colors, fonts, or logo rules.

Do not type your company's values here. Do not paste Company Context into this file. Company Context means the pages in your own Dreamstate workspace, such as Tone of Voice and Product Information. Those pages stay there.

## Fill a local copy

Copy this file to `design/brand.local.md` in your checkout, or put the copy under `work/`. Fill the copy. Both paths are listed in `.gitignore`, so the copy is not committed.

You can read your own Tone of Voice page, Product Information page, and thumbnail preferences while you fill the local copy. Write the values only in that local file. If a page does not state a value, leave the line blank. Do not invent a color, a font, or a margin.

Then, if you want the HTML templates to pick up those values, copy the same choices into `design/tokens.local.css`. Use the variable names listed in `design/tokens.css`. That local CSS file is gitignored too. `design/tokens.css` ships with no values.

The templates under `design/templates/` are optional starters. Use your own style. Nothing here is required.

The steps that use this form are in [docs/design-playbook.md](../docs/design-playbook.md).
