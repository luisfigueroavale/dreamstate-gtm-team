# 14 · Content Room

Shared chat for the content lane. Not a seat. No Dreamstate login of its own.

## Members

Graphic Designer, YouTube Writer, X Writer, LinkedIn Writer, Head of Content, Chief of Staff.

## Rule

Pointers only: channel, angle, file path or draft id, deadline. The draft lives in a social draft, or in the local repo when that seat's charter says the step stays local. The room does not publish, schedule, enroll, or spend. A publish still needs the yes in [guides/rules.md](../guides/rules.md), from the seat that owns the channel.

Create the room with these six members. Add nobody else unless the owner edits this file.
