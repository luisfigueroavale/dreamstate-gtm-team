# Audience folder

Read this file before a content draft, a comment, or a score that judges the reader.

This repo is for any team. The files in `audience/` are blank templates. They hold no company, no reader, and no yes or no answers. Do not type a real reader into these files.

## What each file is for

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `audience/AGENTS.md` | This page. Read order, required files, and when to stop. |
| `audience/ideal-follower.md` | Blank form for the reader: one sentence, firmographics, demographics, psychological profile, and a qualifying test. |

## The reader and the buyer

The ideal follower is the reader. That is the person the content and the comments are for.

The ideal customer is the buyer. The buyer stays in Company Context, on the Ideal Customer page, and in the approved outbound brief. Do not paste that page into this folder. Do not treat the reader and the buyer as the same person unless the owner says they are.

## Local copy

One pattern. Copy the blank file into `audience.local/` and keep the same name.

```text
audience.local/ideal-follower.md
```

`audience.local/` is listed in `.gitignore`. Fill that folder. Do not commit it. Do not keep a second filled copy under `work/` or inside `brand.local/`.

Do not copy this file into `audience.local/`. This page stays the entry point.

## Read order

1. `audience/AGENTS.md` (this file).
2. `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`.

## Required file

Required before a content draft, a social comment, or a lead score that judges the reader:

- `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`

A required file is blank when it is missing, when it is still the untouched template, or when a required section has nothing filled in. A missing or blank required file means stop and ask the owner. Never guess. Do not invent a role, a company type, a fear, or a yes or no answer.

The starter questions in the template are examples of the test's shape. They are not facts about a company. The yes or no answers belong only in the local file.

The owner fills `audience.local/`. A seat does not write those answers into a committed file. Reading the Ideal Customer page does not fill this file.
